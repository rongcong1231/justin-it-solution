HELPFUL ANSWERS BEFORE YOU BUY

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom PC build cost?

A suitable budget depends on your games or software, monitor resolution and preferred components. Send your requirements for an itemised quote.

Can I get a CCTV quotation without an on-site visit?

You can send photos, a simple floor plan and the areas you want monitored for an initial estimate. A site assessment may still be needed before a final installation quote.

Do you help with weak office Wi-Fi and network problems?

We can discuss signal coverage, router or switch configuration, cabling and connection issues. Please share the building layout and symptoms.

Can you repair laptops and printers?

We can discuss common faults, upgrades and printer connectivity or print-quality problems. Please provide the brand, model and symptoms so we can assess the next step.
